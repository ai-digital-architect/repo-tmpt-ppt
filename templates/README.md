# PowerPoint Templates Overview

This directory contains 14 categories of professional PowerPoint templates with strong visuals, inspired by infograpia's comprehensive template library.

## 📂 Directory Structure

Each category folder contains:
- `README.md` - Detailed setup instructions and best practices
- `.pptx` template files (to be added)
- Category-specific guidelines

## 🗂️ Template Categories

### 1. Timelines
**Path**: `timelines/`  
**Purpose**: Showcase events, milestones, or project phases in chronological order  
**Best For**: Project planning, historical presentations, milestone tracking  
**Common Types**: Horizontal timelines, vertical timelines, circular timelines

---

### 2. Strategy, Process & Roadmap
**Path**: `strategy-process-roadmap/`  
**Purpose**: Visualize business strategies, workflows, process steps, and future plans  
**Best For**: Strategic planning, process documentation, product roadmaps  
**Common Types**: Roadmaps, process flows, strategic plans, value chains

---

### 3. Lists & Project Management

## 📖 Getting Started

1. **Identify Your Need**: Determine what type of information you want to present
2. **Select Category**: Choose the category that best matches your content
3. **Read Documentation**: Review the category's README.md for guidelines
4. **Choose Template**: Select a specific template within the category
5. **Customize**: Edit the template to match your content and brand
6. **Present**: Export and share your presentation

---

## 💡 Quick Tips

- **Multiple Categories**: Some content may fit multiple categories - choose based on primary purpose
- **Combination**: Feel free to use slides from multiple categories in one presentation
- **Consistency**: Maintain consistent design elements when combining templates
- **Customization**: All templates are fully editable - adapt to your needs
