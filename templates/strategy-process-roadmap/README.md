# Strategy, Process & Roadmap Templates

## Overview
Templates for visualizing business strategies, workflows, process steps, and future plans. Ideal for strategic planning, process documentation, and communicating roadmaps.

## Use Cases
- Business strategy presentations
- Process flow diagrams
- Product roadmaps
- Workflow documentation
- Strategic planning sessions
- Change management processes

## Setup Instructions

### 1. Download Template
- Place your PowerPoint template files (.pptx) in this directory
- Use descriptive filenames (e.g., `product-roadmap-quarterly.pptx`)

### 2. Customize Template
1. Open the template in Microsoft PowerPoint, Keynote, or Google Slides
2. Replace placeholder steps/phases with your content
3. Adjust process flows and connections
4. Customize colors to represent different streams or priorities
5. Update labels and descriptions

### 3. Design Tips
- Use clear directional arrows to show flow
- Number steps or phases for easy reference
- Group related items with consistent colors
- Use icons to represent different types of activities
- Maintain left-to-right or top-to-bottom flow for readability

### 4. Strategy Template Types
- **Roadmaps**: Product, technology, or organizational roadmaps
- **Process Flows**: Step-by-step workflows and procedures
- **Strategic Plans**: Vision, mission, goals, and initiatives
- **Value Chains**: Business value creation processes

### 5. Export
- Save as .pptx for team collaboration
- Export as PDF for stakeholder distribution
- Use high-resolution PNG for documentation

## Template Naming Convention
Use the format: `[type]-[timeframe/variant].pptx`
- Examples: `roadmap-product-quarterly.pptx`, `process-workflow-5steps.pptx`

## Best Practices
- Keep process flows simple and avoid overcrowding
- Use consistent symbols for start/end/decision points
- Include legends for complex diagrams
- Test comprehension with a colleague before presenting
- Use swimlanes for cross-functional processes
